ADR 0006: personal conversations and explicit family sharing

Date: 2026-10-29

Status: accepted future product direction and access constraints; deferred beyond the initial owner-only Telegram integration in ADR 0007. The owner confirmed Agent Platform is unavailable on their account. No family service or two-account deployment is accepted.

Context

The owner selected personal conversations for both household members with a shared family space. Agent Platform can provide creator-bound personal chats without a dedicated bot number, but offers no group chat and is not available on every account. The earlier Baileys preparation remains inactive; it is not the selected deployment path.

Decision

Use separate authenticated personal entry points and separate private conversation state. Share only explicitly created/shared family objects through a service that enforces ownership and membership outside model prompts. A shared Nora identity does not grant access to another member’s private history, files, credentials or integrations.

Treat Agent Platform as the candidate transport, gated on both accounts' actual eligibility. Each connection owns its token and polling state; replies/notifications use that recipient’s own connection. Keep family storage and its authorization independent of transport. Profiles alone are not an access boundary. No private-message relay or native WhatsApp group is included in this decision.

Consequences

The existing single-profile no-tools coordinator is retained until a bounded multi-user runtime and negative privacy tests pass. Extra agent connections do not automatically create shared memory or a family application. Missing Agents UI blocks live enrollment but not design or mocked transport assessment. See the design, evidence boundaries and acceptance order.
