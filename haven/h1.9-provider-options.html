H1.9: Storage and monitoring options

Checked against official sources on 2026-10-30. The owner requested alternatives before selecting a provider. This is a comparison, not an order or an account-specific quote. Prices below describe storage only, before taxes unless stated; USD and EUR are deliberately not converted. Volume means the entire retained repository, not the size of one daily backup. Confirm billable rounding, minimum invoice/payment rules and actual account terms before ordering.

Backup storage

Candidate Listed pricing / example Location and access Fit and open checks

Scaleway Standard Multi-AZ

EUR 0.01606/GB-month; 20 GB approximately EUR 0.3212 before tax, EUR 0.3887 with illustrative 21% VAT. Requests/ingress included; 75 GB monthly egress allowance, then listed EUR 0.01/GB. Do not budget on a temporary trial.

EU provider, Paris candidate; S3 backend.

Keeps the earlier EU-provider direction. Verify IAM/bucket-policy interaction, protected recovery points and account billing.

Cloudflare R2 Standard

10 GB-month storage, 1M Class A and 10M Class B operations/month included; then USD 0.015/GB-month and request charges. 20 GB storage line item approximately USD 0.15. Internet egress is free under the published conditions.

US provider; explicit EU jurisdiction is available, with its jurisdiction-specific S3 endpoint. A location hint alone is not the same guarantee.

Potentially no storage charge for a small retained repository within free limits. Verify jurisdiction, token scope, compatibility and object-retention behavior; no actual R2 account/API test has run.

Backblaze B2 pay-as-you-go

USD 6.95/TB per 30 days; conservative 20 GB calculation USD 0.139 before any allowance/tax. Standard API calls are free under the current offering; direct egress allowance is 3x average stored data, then USD 0.01/GB.

US provider with EU Central in Amsterdam. Region is chosen when creating the account and cannot subsequently be changed on that account. S3 or B2 access.

Low unit price and a backup-oriented service. Confirm current account allowances/billing and restricted application-key/retention semantics. No free-storage allowance is assumed in this conservative estimate.

Hetzner Storage Box BX11

Official page’s hidden pricing label lists EUR 3.20/month excluding VAT; minimum capacity 1 TB. At 20 GB the whole plan is still required. Confirm the localized order price.

EU provider, Germany/Finland; SFTP backend, subaccounts and provider snapshots.

More capacity and a separate snapshot control plane, at a higher fixed floor for a tiny pilot. Storage Box is not an S3 bucket; data resides on one host with RAID. Verify subaccount restrictions and independently controlled snapshots.

Backblaze’s 2026-03-17 update establishes the May 1 pricing/API change; older help pages about per-call charges should not override it. The primary pricing page’s free-storage wording was not established by the retrieved live page, so the table does not subtract it.

For a small Haven-only repository, compare Scaleway when an EU-operated service matters most, R2 EU when staying within a free allowance matters most, and B2 EU for its storage pricing. Storage Box becomes more interesting when the owner also wants substantially more backup capacity. These are tradeoffs; credentials, deletion protection and an actual restore still require acceptance on whichever provider is selected.

Independent monitoring

Candidate Verified public offer Fit

Healthchecks.io Hobbyist

USD 0/month, 20 jobs, 100 log entries per job.

Focused dead-man heartbeat monitoring; explicit failure signals are supported. Two checks can cover backup completion and a combined service/backup-freshness heartbeat. Keep ping URLs private and send no raw logs.

Better Stack Uptime

10 monitors, 10 heartbeats and one status page with three-minute checks advertised free.

An alternative if one console should also cover other public services. Heartbeats still work without exposing Haven’s private dashboard. Account-specific notification/integration limits remain to verify.

A monitor installed only on the Haven VPS cannot report that host’s complete disappearance without an external receiver. This pilot does not add another self-hosted server just to run monitoring. Before accepting either hosted candidate, choose the owner’s notification destination, confirm a real alert and exercise a deliberately missed heartbeat without disabling production access.
